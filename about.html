Built by pet people, right here in Hyderabad.

Pawsense began with a simple question: what if our pets could tell us how they’re doing? We’re a small pet-tech team making a collar that listens, so you can spend less time worrying and more time playing.

Our story

Like many Hyderabad families, we share our homes with pets who think they own the place. Between hot afternoons, crowded roads and apartment gates that never quite stay shut, we were always a little anxious when ours wandered out of sight.

So we started building a collar that could keep quiet watch: one that was light enough for a cat, tough enough for a Labrador and clear enough that anyone in the family could use it.

What we believe

Comfort first

A soft, lightweight collar your pet forgets they’re wearing.

Clear, not noisy

Simple updates that tell you what matters, when it matters.

Your pet’s data is yours

We only use health and location data to help you care for your pet.

Tested on real Hyderabad walks

Our prototypes have been through lakeside evening walks, midday heat, rain-soaked lanes and long apartment corridors. Every feature is shaped by how pets and families actually live in this city.
