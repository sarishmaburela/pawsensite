Features that fit Hyderabad life

Four ways Pawsense helps you look after your pet, all from one smart collar and one simple app.

Live GPS tracking

Follow your pet’s location in real time and set safe zones around home, the park or the groomer. If your pet steps outside a zone, you’ll know straight away.

Health monitoring

Keep an eye on heart rate, rest and body warmth. During Hyderabad’s hottest months, you’ll get a reminder when it’s time for shade and water.

Hunger detection

Pawsense learns your pet’s routine and reminds you when mealtime is near, which is handy when the family’s schedules don’t line up.

Activity analytics

See how much your pet walks, plays and sleeps, and set small goals for healthier days. Share weekly reports with your vet in one tap.
